The heart of our work

No family should face cancer alone.

Merciful Ministry was formed to stand beside cancer patients and their families in Egypt, especially when the cost of treatment puts proper care out of reach.

Rooted in Christian service, the ministry combines spiritual pastoral support with practical help—meeting people with compassion, respect, and prayer.

Our story

A personal experience became a way to serve.

In a 2020 account of the ministry’s beginnings, Hegumen Fr. Louka Sidarous described how his experience with pancreatic cancer led him to think about serving other cancer patients. He saw two needs: spiritual pastoral care and help easing the burdens of treatment costs.

That desire grew into a ministry bringing clergy, medical professionals, and servants together for patients and families in Egypt.

The service grows

A quiet act of mercy became a growing ministry.

Even in illness and suffering, Hegumen Fr. Louka Sidarous kept the needs of others close to his heart. Having experienced the pain of illness himself, he wanted to ease the burdens of fellow patients and their families.

That compassion gave shape to the Father Bishoy Kamel Ministry for Cancer Patients. Its purpose was practical and personal: help cover medical expenses so families would not carry a financial burden on top of illness. Fr. Louka encouraged the fathers to seek out those most in need, especially families enduring hardship quietly.

He asked that assistance be offered discreetly—where possible, by paying treatment expenses directly and leaving the family with a reminder of Christ’s love. By God’s grace, the ministry has continued to grow and reach people in many places. We give thanks for all who sustain this work, and pray that God will continue to move hearts to serve.
